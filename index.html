MANNY'S HOUSE APPAREL

WE DON'T MAKE INVENTORY. WE MAKE EDITIONS.

Some ideas shouldn't live forever.

Manny's House Apparel is where the ideas, stories and statements we believe are worth making physical become something you can actually own.

01

THE RULE

25 PIECES. ONE RUN.

Every design released by Manny's House Apparel is produced once — in an edition of only 25 pieces.

No restocks.
No second run.
No bringing it back because it sold.

When the 25 are gone, that edition is finished.

02

THE LINES

DIFFERENT STORIES. SAME RULE.

Manny's House Apparel is built in lines — each with its own point of view, visual language and reason for existing.

The lines will change.
The designs will change.
The stories will change.

The rule won't.

03

CURRENT LINE / CENSORED

THE PART OF THE STORY PEOPLE LEAVE OUT.

Everybody loves the story of being self-made.

What gets censored are the people who helped. The opportunities that opened doors. The sacrifices somebody else made. The advantages we inherited. The second chances we were given.

Once success arrives, those parts of the story have a funny way of disappearing.

Censored is about putting them back in.

04

THE IMPACT

WE DON'T ALL START AT THE SAME PLACE.

One-third of the profits from every Manny's House Apparel line is given back to local nonprofits and community organizations helping people facing inequality, limited opportunity, civil-rights challenges and barriers to simply living with dignity.

The organizations may change from line to line.

The commitment doesn't.

25 PIECES. ONE RUN. ONE-THIRD GIVEN BACK.
