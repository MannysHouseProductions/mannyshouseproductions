MANNY'S HOUSE APPAREL

WE DON'T MAKE INVENTORY. WE MAKE EDITIONS.

Manny's House Apparel is where the ideas, stories and statements we believe are worth making physical become something you can actually own.

Every piece starts with a reason to exist.

02

THE RULE

25 PIECES. ONE RUN.

Every design released by Manny's House Apparel is produced once — in an edition of only twenty-five pieces.

No restocks.
No second run.
No bringing it back because it sold.

When the 25 are gone, that edition is finished.

03

THE LINES

DIFFERENT STORIES. SAME RULE.

Manny's House Apparel is built in lines — each with its own point of view, visual language and reason for existing.

The current line is Censored.

Censored is about the parts of the story people leave out — the help, opportunity, access, sacrifice and support that helped make success possible.

Nobody starts from the same place. Nobody builds anything completely alone.

04

THE IMPACT

1 OF 25. THEN IT'S GONE.

Every line is limited by design.

One-third of the profits from every Manny's House Apparel line is given back to local nonprofits and community organizations supporting people facing inequality, limited opportunity, civil-rights challenges and barriers to living with dignity.

The organization may change from line to line.

The commitment doesn't.

25 PIECES. ONE RUN. ONE-THIRD GIVEN BACK.
